fn estimate_transaction_budget( &self, transaction: &Transaction, ) -> impl Future<Output = Result<Option<u64>, Self::Error>>

Estimate the gas budget needed for a transaction, typically by simulating it and reading the gas cost from the result. Ok(None) means no estimate is available; finish then fails unless a budget was set explicitly.
